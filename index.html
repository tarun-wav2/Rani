My dearest Rani(My hunny), 💗🫶🏻,

Happy Birthday to the person jo dheere-dheere meri life ka ek bahut special part ban gayi. 🥹✨ Honestly, mujhe nahi pata tha ki ek friendship itni saari memories, hasi-mazaak aur chhoti-chhoti baaton se itni special ban sakti hai. 🩷🦋

Pata hai, teri sabse achhi baat kya hai? Teri woh chhoti-chhoti baatein, tera bachpana 😂, teri nautanki 🤌🏻, bina baat ke gussa hona 😤 aur phir khud hi normal ho jaana. Tujhse baat karke kabhi hasi aati hai, kabhi irritation hoti hai 😂, par tere saath har conversation ki apni ek alag vibe hoti hai. 🫶🏻🌷

Kabhi-kabhi humari baatein kam ho jaati hain, kabhi conversations beech mein hi ruk jaati hain, but that doesn't change the fact ki tu meri life ki ek achhi aur special friend hai. 🥹💗 Har friendship mein roz ghanton baat hona zaroori nahi hota; kabhi-kabhi chhoti si baat aur ek simple si reel bhi smile karwa deti hai. 🌸✨

Aaj tere birthday par bas itna kehna hai ki tu hamesha khush reh, apne saare dreams poore kar, life mein bahut aage badh aur apni cute si smile kabhi mat khona. 👑💖 Jab bhi life difficult lage, yaad rakhna ki tu jitna sochti hai usse kahin zyada capable hai. 🫂🌻

Aur haan madam ji 😂, apna khayal rakha kar, time par khaya kar, zyada overthinking mat kiya kar aur apni health ko ignore bilkul mat karna. 😤💗 Ye saari daant birthday wale din bhi free mein milegi! 🤣🫶🏻

Thank you meri life mein ek achhi friendship aur itni saari pyari memories add karne ke liye. 🥹🌷 I hope this year brings you endless happiness, beautiful surprises and countless reasons to smile. 🎂🎁✨

Happy Birthday once again, Rani! 👑💗

Hamesha khush rehna, apni favourite wali smile ke saath. 🦋🌸 Aur haan, aaj ke din full nakhre allowed hain, birthday girl jo hai! 😂🎀

With lots of wishes, care and dher saari friendship wali pyaari si feelings, 🫶🏻💖

Your friend, always cheering for you. 🌷✨
